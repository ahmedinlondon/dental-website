Comprehensive Orthodontic Care

Orthodontic treatment is a carefully planned journey designed to create healthy, confident smiles at any age. At our clinic, we provide comprehensive consultations using digital scans, X-rays, and clinical photography to develop fully personalised treatment plans.

We offer a full range of modern orthodontic solutions, including traditional metal braces, ceramic braces, lingual braces, and clear aligner therapy for discreet treatment. For growing patients, functional appliances may be used to guide jaw development, while adults benefit from aesthetic and minimally visible options.

Throughout treatment, we provide regular monitoring, adjustments, and emergency care when needed. After treatment is complete, we ensure long-term stability with custom retainers and follow-up care.

Braces or Aligners?

Which option is right for you?

Braces

Best for complex cases that require detailed control, significant tooth movement, or bite correction. Available in metal or ceramic options.

  • Highly effective for all case types
  • Fixed appliances work continuously
  • Excellent control for complex movements

Aligners

Best for mild to moderate alignment issues. Discreet, removable, and designed for comfort and lifestyle flexibility.

  • Nearly invisible
  • Removable for eating and cleaning
  • Smooth and comfortable
